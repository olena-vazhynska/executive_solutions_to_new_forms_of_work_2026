      How to use this library

      Three habits that make the difference in the Scheme Design Lab.

      • Check the date and the scope. Coverage statistics and legal positions in this field age in months, not years.
      • Read a judgment for its test, not its result. The test is the part you can transplant into your own legal order.
      • Pair every standard with an administrative question. A rule that cannot be registered, collected or appealed is not yet a solution.
